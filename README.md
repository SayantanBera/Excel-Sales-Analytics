# 📊 Excel Sales & Finance Analytics Project 



## 🔍 Project Overview



This project focuses on building dynamic **Sales and Finance Analytics Reports** using Excel tools such as Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX. It demonstrates the ability to transform raw data into meaningful insights that support business decision-making." 



---



## 📈 Sales Analytics



### 🎯 Objectives



* Develop a **Customer Performance Report** to evaluate customer-level sales trends.

* Perform a comparative analysis between **Market Performance and Sales Targets**.



### 📌 Key Insights Delivered



* Identification of high-performing and underperforming customers.

* Analysis of sales trends and seasonal patterns.

* Tracking of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as revenue growth and target achievement.



### 💡 Business Impact



* Helps in optimizing **customer discount strategies**.

* Supports **data-driven negotiation decisions**.

* Identifies **expansion opportunities** in high-potential markets.



---



## 💰 Finance Analytics



### 🎯 Objectives



* Create Profit & Loss (P&L) reports:



  * By **Fiscal Year**

  * By **Month**

  * By **Market**



### 📌 Key Insights Delivered



* Clear visibility into revenue, costs, and profitability.

* Performance comparison across different time periods and regions.



### 💡 Business Impact



* Enables **financial performance evaluation**.

* Assists in **budgeting and forecasting**.

* Strengthens **stakeholder communication and confidence**.
